=== STEP 2 - REMOVE THE MOUTH (the main manipulation) ===
Make an elliptical selection centred at (1286, 2282) with radii 338 x 201,
feather about 20 px, then Edit > Content-Aware Fill (or Fill > Content-Aware).

skin from the forehead and cheek. Then Filter > Noise > Add Noise at about
2.5%, Gaussian, Monochromatic, masked to that area only, so the healed skin has
the same texture as the rest of the face and does not look plastic.
Keep a hidden copy of the untouched mouth as its own layer named
"Original mouth - BEFORE (toggle me)" so the edit can be switched on and off.

=== STEP 3 - GRADE ===
Curves adjustment layer, control points (input -> output):
  0->0, 60->10, 110->26, 145->58, 175->142, 205->196, 230->242, 255->255

Large soft radial vignette centred around (1275, 2120), darkening the edges by
about 75%.

Bottom scrim: a new layer with a linear gradient, transparent at y=2680 down to

=== STEP 5 - THE NEW SKILL: DISPLACEMENT MAP ===
This is the technique I have to show I learned independently.
Duplicate the portrait layer, Image > Adjustments > Desaturate, then
Filter > Blur > Gaussian Blur at about 30 px. Save that as its own .psd
(e.g. facemap.psd), then hide/delete the duplicate.
Select the whole autocomplete bar group, convert it to a Smart Object, then
Filter > Distort > Displace with Horizontal Scale 20, Vertical Scale 11,
Stretch To Fit, Repeat Edge Pixels, and choose facemap.psd.
The bar should then bend over the cheeks and jaw instead of sitting flat.
Also add a soft shading layer over the bar set to Multiply so it picks up the
same light as the face.
